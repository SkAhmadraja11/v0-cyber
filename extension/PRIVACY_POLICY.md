# Privacy Policy - Phishing Detective Enterprise

**Last Updated:** February 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Phishing Detective Enterprise ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide our security services, we process the following data:
*   **URLs and Page Content:** When you visit a website, the extension may send the URL and a snippet of the page content to our analysis engine to determine if it is safe.
*   **Technical Data:** We may collect basic technical information such as your browser version and extension version for debugging purposes.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
*   **Threat Detection:** The primary use of data is to analyze websites for phishing, malware, and other security threats.
*   **Service Improvement:** Aggregated, anonymized scan results help us improve our detection algorithms.
*   **Usage Limits:** We store a local counter of your daily scans to enforce service limits (e.g., free tier quotas).

## 4. Data Retention
*   **Transient Processing:** URLs sent for analysis are processed in real-time. We do not build a browsing history profile of our users.
*   **Local Storage:** Your preferences and daily quota counters are stored locally on your device using Chrome's Storage API.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We share data only with:
*   **Security Partners:** In some cases, anonymized threat indicators (e.g., a confirmed malicious URL) may be shared with security databases to protect the broader community.

## 6. Your Consent
By using Phishing Detective Enterprise, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at support@phishingdetective.com.
